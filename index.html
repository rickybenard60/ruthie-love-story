FOR RUTHIE

I made this for you...

Ruthie, come a little closer.

Sio message ya kawaida.
Ni little story niliyotaka uisome mwenyewe, bila haraka.

Take your time.

CHAPTER ONE

Kuna vitu muda hauondoi.

“Baadhi ya memories hazihitaji kurudi ili ziendelee kuwa nzuri.”

Kuna watu tunakutana nao, tunaishi nao kwenye kipindi fulani cha maisha, halafu maisha yanaendelea.

Lakini kuna baadhi ya watu ambao wakishawahi kuwa muhimu, kumbukumbu zao haziondoki kwa sababu tu muda umepita.

Na, Ruthie... wewe ni mmoja wao.

CHAPTER TWO

Lakini hebu... tusitoke serious sana. 😂

Maana nikianza kuwa serious sana, utasema nimeandika essay. 😌

♥ OFFICIAL COMPLAINT

Ruthie, nina complaint moja dhidi yako.

Ulikuwa na tabia ya kufanya moments za kawaida zionekane special bila hata kujaribu.

“Na ukimfanya mtu acheke mara nyingi, usishangae akianza kukukumbuka kuliko ulivyopanga.” 😂

Sasa angalia nilivyoishia... niko hapa natengeneza website badala ya kujifanya nimekusahau.

Very unfair, Ruthie. ❤️

Case status: Still under investigation. 😂
CHAPTER THREE

Niliacha vitu vitatu hapa.

Usifungue zote kwa haraka... chagua moja kwanza. 😌

01

Tabasamu lako

Kuna aina ya smiles ambazo mtu hazihitaji kuzilazimisha.

Gusa hapa...

Kuna siku niligundua kwamba smile yako ilikuwa na uwezo wa kubadilisha kabisa mood ya conversation.

Na hilo lilikuwa jambo nililolipenda kuhusu wewe. ❤️

02

Kicheko chako

Kitu fulani kuhusu kicheko chako kilifanya moments za kawaida zionekane tofauti.

Gusa hapa...

Ukweli ni kwamba, mara nyingine nilikuwa nasubiri tu uscheke.

Because somehow, your laugh made everything lighter. 😌

03

Wewe tu

Si lazima niwe na sababu kubwa kila wakati. Sometimes... mtu ni special tu.

Gusa hapa...

Na labda hii ndiyo ilikuwa rahisi zaidi lakini ngumu zaidi kusema.

You were simply someone I cared about. ❤️

CHAPTER FOUR
♥

Kama ningesema jambo moja tu...

Ruthie,

Sikukusogelea hapa kwa sababu nataka kubadilisha yaliyopita.

Nilitaka tu nikukumbushe kwamba kulikuwa na kipindi ambacho uwepo wako ulikuwa sehemu nzuri sana ya maisha yangu.

Kuna vitu tulivyocheka, vitu tulivyoongea, na moments ambazo wakati huo zilionekana ndogo...

lakini leo nikifikiria, najikuta natabasamu. ❤️

Na labda hicho ndicho kitu nilichotaka kukupa leo: smile moja ndogo.

No pressure. No expectations. Just one beautiful memory.

ONE LAST QUESTION

Ruthie... umetabasamu? 👀

ONE MORE THING

So, Ruthie... this was for you. ❤️
